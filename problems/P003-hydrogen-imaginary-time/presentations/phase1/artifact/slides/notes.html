H1 · p. 4

E₀ = −ln Z / β at large β; the trace closes the paths

Closed paths: correct. For hydrogen Z = ∞, so E₀ has to come from the kernel at a point

H2 · p. 5

Split each slice; valid for small Δβ

Fails near r = 0 for every Δβ. The justification is the Trotter formula, which needs V bounded below

H3 · p. 6

The bead formula for Z

Δβ V in place of βV; in three dimensions the power 3m/2 and vector positions

H4 · p. 8

The weight tends to +∞ as r → 0

Correct, but not enough alone: 1/r is unbounded and still integrable

H5 · p. 8

r² cannot compensate the weight

Correct, and now proved, with the exact growth law

H6 · p. 9

The kinetic term does not compensate

Correct, and now proved: the springs do not change the leading growth. The large-volume divergence was missing

Hydrogen from imaginary time · Phase 1 · 14

Putting it together for our notes. The closed paths of page four are right, but for hydrogen Z is infinite, so ground-state dominance cannot be applied to it directly. The Taylor argument of page five fails near the nucleus. Page six needs delta beta on the potential and, for hydrogen, three dimensions. The claims of pages eight and nine are all correct; we had asserted them, and they are now proved. What we missed is the second divergence, from large distances.
